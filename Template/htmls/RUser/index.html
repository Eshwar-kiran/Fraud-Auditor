Identifying Health Insurance Claim Frauds

Using Mixture of Clinical Concepts

Patients depend on health insurance provided by government systems, private organizations, or both to utilize expensive healthcare services. This dependency creates opportunities for fraudulent insurance claims, causing billions of dollars in losses every year.

This project proposes a Machine Learning based fraud detection system using diagnosis and procedure codes. The system learns meaningful clinical representations and predicts fraudulent insurance claims using algorithms such as Support Vector Machine, Logistic Regression, Decision Tree, and Voting Classifier.
